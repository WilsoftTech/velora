## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, high-precision dark-mode experience tailored for enterprise AI and modern technology platforms. Rooted in deep midnight navy and luminous photonic blues, the visual identity communicates technical authority, machine-learning intelligence, and around-the-clock computational reliability.

The aesthetic integrates crisp **Minimalism** with controlled **Glassmorphism**. Key characteristics include:
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Immersive void-space backgrounds layered with subtle linear ambient glows and translucent glass containers.
- **Electric Illumination**: High-contrast cobalt and sky-blue accents that command visual hierarchy against deep abyssal slates.
- **Precision Engineering**: Razor-sharp micro-borders, clean geometric typography, and calibrated spacing that reflect enterprise readiness and clarity.
- **Tactile Modernity**: Elevated interactive surfaces featuring delicate outer borders, soft radial backdrops, and focused glow states.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for immersive, high-legibility dark environments:

- **Surface & Background Architecture**:
  - `canvas-default`: `#070B14` (Deep obsidian navy representing primary canvas foundation).
  - `canvas-subtle`: `#0A0F1D` (Lifted background for nested sections and contrast bands).
  - `surface-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (Primary card backdrop with backdrop blur filter).
  - `surface-glass-hover`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Active state for interactive panels).
  - `border-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` (Subtle, crisp card outline).
  - `border-accent`: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)` (Glow-focused active edge).

- **Brand & Accent Spectrum**:
  - `primary`: `#2563EB` (Cobalt Royal Blue for prominent call-to-actions and focal actions).
  - `primary-hover`: `#1D4ED8` (Deepened cobalt for pressed/hover feedback).
  - `secondary`: `#38BDF8` (Luminous Sky Blue for status pills, badge borders, and keyword highlights).
  - `tertiary`: `#60A5FA` (Electric highlight for feature badges, glow shadows, and focused text).

- **Typography & Neutral Tokens**:
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (Ultra-high contrast, primary headlines and key values).
  - `text-secondary`: `#94A3B8` (Slate tint for readable body copy, descriptions, and metadata).
  - `text-muted`: `#64748B` (Subtle captions, inactive tab labels, and footer meta).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers contemporary geometric precision with natural humanist balance, ideal for scanning data-heavy interfaces and high-impact hero statements.

- **Headlines & Display**: Bold, tight-tracked letterforms with negative kerning emphasize technological authority and impact.
- **Feature Highlighting**: Key narrative phrases within hero headers may alternate between `text-primary` (`#FFFFFF`) and electric blue gradients (`#38BDF8` to `#2563EB`) to direct gaze.
- **Eyebrows & Overlines**: Uppercase tagging using `label-tag` or `label-md` with expanded letter spacing establishes technical metadata contexts (e.g., category badges, system indicators).
- **Body & Captions**: Optimized line heights and neutral slate coloring prevent ocular fatigue during sustained sessions in dark mode.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid structured with strict mathematical cadence based on an 8px base rhythm.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, dynamic section margin up to 1280px max-width container. Asymmetric split layouts (e.g., 5-column copy to 7-column media card) anchor complex AI dashboards and device mockups.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with 20px gutters. Multi-column showcase cards compress into 2-column or stacked arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single flow, 16px (`1rem`) gutters and outer margin. Complex preview panes reflow cleanly below lead actions.
- **Vertical Hierarchy**: Generous section spacing (`space-2xl`) prevents glass layers and luminous ambient halos from competing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered semi-transparent materials, calibrated edge luminescence, and volumetric chromatic shadows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: Matte `#070B14` canvas ground with optional linear atmospheric radial gradients (e.g., `radial-gradient(ellipse at 80% 40%, rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12) 0%, transparent 60%)`).
- **Layer 1 (Glass Container Panels)**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surrounded by a 1px composite outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Modals)**: Lifted surfaces use `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with a multi-stop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15);`
- **Active & Glow Highlights**: Focus rings and primary callout edges employ luminous blue radiance (`0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The system implements clean, rounded geometric contours balancing modern sleekness with ergonomic digital software standards:

- **Default Radii (Level 2 - 8px / 0.5rem)**: Standard buttons, input controls, pill indicators, and dropdown popovers.
- **Large Radii (16px / 1rem)**: Card surfaces, interactive glass preview modules, and modal containers.
- **Full Radii (Pill / 9999px)**: Category tags, status pills, and system micro-chips (e.g., "• AI ASSISTANTS").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` background with white text, font weight 600, 10px 24px padding, 8px border radius. Hover introduces background tint `#1D4ED8` and subtle halo `0 0 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Semi-transparent dark slate surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), white text. Hover state upgrades border to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)` and background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Glass Cards & Presentation Panels
- Container with `border-radius: 16px`, `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, and internal padding of `1.5rem` to `2.5rem`.
- Supports integrated status headers, internal sub-cards (`background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`), and inner accent borders.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Pill Eyebrow**: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8`, uppercase `11px` weight 700 with an animated or solid radiant dot (`#38BDF8`).
- **Feature Spec Micro-Chips**: Icon paired with short bold titles and secondary descriptions aligned horizontally in footer strips.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background `rgba(10, 15, 29, 0.8)` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focused state applies border color `#38BDF8` with a diffused glow box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`.

### Feature & Check Lists
- Bulleted technical perks utilize circular tinted check icons (`#38BDF8`) encased in a soft glow circle `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` alongside medium-weight white body copy.
